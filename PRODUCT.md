# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

HES serves mixed website traffic: industrial clients and workers connected to electrical installations in Germany, residential clients in Croatia looking for finishing works or household electrical installations, buyers considering Loxone smart-home components and installation, and tradespeople or self-builders browsing tool rental.

## Product Purpose

The site explains Hranj Electrical Services d.o.o. as a multi-line electrical and construction business, routes visitors to the right service or catalogue, presents Loxone and tool inventory with prices, and converts visitors through direct contact, quote requests, cart enquiries, or job applications.

## Brand Personality

Technical, practical, assured. The interface should feel competent and electrically precise without becoming cold, and should support the positioning that a fair price can still mean high quality.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic contractor templates, vague stock-business polish, decorative tech effects that obscure the actual offer, and catalogue experiences that bury prices or make two different inventories feel like one confused shop.

## Design Principles

Lead with routing: visitors arrive with different intents, so the page should quickly separate services, Loxone buying, tool rental, contact, and recruitment.

Keep claims grounded: use only stated capabilities, partner status, inventory, prices, and contact routes; do not invent guarantees, service radii, reviews, or certifications.

Make browsing efficient: catalogues need clear categories, search, summaries, stable product cards, and direct actions.

Preserve HES's engineered atmosphere: motion, texture, and dark/light theming should feel like electrical infrastructure, not decoration.

Protect practical access: core content should remain readable, theme-aware, keyboard accessible, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve visible focus states, semantic headings and landmarks, responsive layouts, skip links, and reduced-motion alternatives. Interactive catalogue and cart features should fail gracefully enough that contact remains reachable.
