HES works across three activities: industrial electrical installations mainly in Germany; finishing construction works and residential electrical installations in Croatia; sale and installation of Loxone smart-home components in Croatia. Its stated specialisms are electrical installations and Loxone smart-home systems, covering design, assembly and commissioning. Croatian finishing work is offered turnkey ("ključ u ruke"). It also rents professional tools.

## 8. Conflicts & ambiguities

1. Number of activities: services are split into three categories, yet tool rental is a fourth activity and the legacy text sells it as a service. Its rank is undefined.
2. Germany, industrial vs. residential: German work is "isključivo" industrial, but another line offers "kućne i industrijske instalacije" without naming a market.
3. Turnkey scope: "ključ u ruke" is stated only for finishing works. Does it cover electrical and Loxone work too?
4. Mechanical assembly (mehaničke montaže) appears only in the outdated legacy text. Still offered?
5. Waldner: named as a partner, but the partnership, products and business line are never explained. Used only in the legacy text, as an automation credibility claim.
6. Loxone is presented both as a specialism and as the third-ranked, smallest activity.
7. Contact: only alen.hranj@hes.hr and one mobile number; no person's name, role or address.
8. Pricing basis: rental per day, Loxone per unit. VAT, minimum rental period, deposits and delivery unstated.
9. Catalog completeness: unclear whether 16 rental and 59 Loxone items are the full catalogs or an excerpt.
